# Cover geometry references

Owner correction: Redmi 14C and Tecno Spark 30C stock cases use FOUR separate circular openings arranged top, left, right, bottom within a circular raised area. The owner's attached photo overrides the earlier generic full-island opening. The three `-v3.png` assets match this stock layout; prompts are in `stock-layout-prompts.json`.

Researched 9 October 2026. Images used only to inspect phone geometry, not licensed for display or published. Website visuals are original built-in image generation outputs. - Galaxy A16: three separate equal circular camera openings vertically down the upper left, plus one tiny flash hole to their right between top and middle. Rounded corners.
- Galaxy A55 5G: three separate equal circular camera openings vertically down the upper left, plus one tiny flash hole to their right between top and middle. Rounded corners.
- Galaxy A35 5G: three separate equal circular camera openings vertically down the upper left, plus one tiny flash hole to their right between top and middle. Rounded corners.
- Galaxy A52: one rounded rectangular upper-left camera island opening roughly 33% phone width and 28% phone height, fitting three vertical lenses on left plus fourth small lens and flash to their right. Rounded phone corners.
- Galaxy A71: one rounded rectangular upper-left camera island opening roughly 29% phone width and 23% phone height, fitting four lenses in L formation and flash. Rounded phone corners.
- Galaxy S24: three separate circular camera holes vertically upper left plus tiny flash hole to right between top and middle. Compact phone rounded corners.
- Galaxy S24 Ultra: EXACTLY six separate circular OPEN holes upper left: THREE LARGE equal holes in vertical left column; TWO SMALLER holes in right column level with top and middle large holes; ONE TINY flash hole between those two small holes. Five module openings plus one flash opening. Sharp nearly square corners, tall wide flat sides. No big combined camera rectangle.
- Galaxy S23 Ultra: EXACTLY six separate circular OPEN holes upper left: THREE LARGE equal holes in vertical left column; TWO SMALLER holes in right column level with top and middle large holes; ONE TINY flash hole between those two small holes. Five module openings plus one flash opening. Sharp nearly square corners, slightly rounded long sidewalls. No combined rectangle.
- Galaxy S20: one narrow vertically elongated rounded-rectangle camera opening upper left roughly25% phone width and23% height, for three lenses and flash. Rounded corners.
- Galaxy Note 20 Ultra: one wide vertically elongated rounded-rectangular camera opening upper left about37% phone width and29% height, for three large lenses left and flash/laser right. Sharp nearly square phone corners.
- Pop 9: one large rounded-square camera island opening upper left, about50% phone width and28% phone height, matching diagonal two-ring camera layout and flash. Rounded phone corners. Use the Pop9 4G reference.
- Spark 30C: one LARGE circular camera island opening centred horizontally near top, diameter about70% phone width; match OFFICIAL Tecno circular island, not Samsung rectangles. Rounded phone corners.
- Hot 50i: one rounded-square upper-left camera island opening about51% phone width and28% height, for two vertically stacked rounded-square lenses left and tall flash pill right. Rounded corners.
- 14C: one LARGE circular camera island opening centred horizontally near top, diameter about70% phone width. Rounded phone corners.
